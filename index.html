Retrieval-Augmented Memory

RAG is usually framed as a search problem. For companions, it is a relationship problem.

You are not just fetching facts; you are fetching the moments that make the current turn land. Ranking by relevance alone misses emotional salience.

The best retrieval blends semantic similarity with recency and importance, so the character brings up what a friend would bring up.

Retrieve the moment, not just the document.
